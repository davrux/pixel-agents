# Art credits and asset licences

Some of the art in `assets/` is derived from third-party pixel-art packs. The
packs themselves are **not** in this repository — only the art derived from
them by the importers under `server/scripts/` (`gen-metro-*.mts`,
`gen-sprout-*.mts`), which read the pack from a gitignored `tmp/` directory.

## MetroCity — JIK-A-4

<https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack>

The characters, the `floor-metro-*` / `wall-metro-*` sets and the
`furniture-metro-*` tilesets are derived from this pack.

## Sprout Lands, Basic pack — Cup Nooble

<https://cupnooble.itch.io/sprout-lands-asset-pack>

Assets — from: *Sprout Lands* — by: **Cup Nooble**.

Derived from it: the `floor-sprout-*` and `wall-sprout-*` sets
(`assets/floors/sprout_*.png`, `assets/walls/wall_sprout.png`) and the
`furniture-sprout-*` tilesets (`assets/tiled/png/furniture/sprout/`).

The pack's own terms, which travel with that art:

- **Non-commercial use only.** No use in NFT projects, and no AI training.
- The assets may be modified, and open-source projects may redistribute them
  as part of the project — but **the pack itself may not be redistributed or
  resold**, modified or not.
- **Credit to Cup Nooble is required.**
